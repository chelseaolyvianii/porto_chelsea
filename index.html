Mangrove Aboveground Carbon (AGC) Estimation & Feature Ablation Study (ICIMTECH 2026)

PythonXGBoostScikit-learn

To evaluate the contribution of Sentinel-2 optical features and GEDI LiDAR structural features in predicting above-ground carbon biomass (AGC) in the mangroves of Rinca Island. Testing the effectiveness of optical data as a substitute for LiDAR and identifying the optimal feature combination using XGBoost regression, with the best results obtained from the GLCM + LiDAR combination (R² = 0.8779)

  • Conference Paper Published at ICIMTech 2026 proceeding (Scopus-indexed)
  • Ablation study of Sentinel-2 optical vs. GEDI LiDAR structural features
  • Study area: mangroves of Rinca Island
  • Tests whether optical data can substitute for LiDAR
  • XGBoost regression; best combination GLCM + LiDAR (R² = 0.8779)
